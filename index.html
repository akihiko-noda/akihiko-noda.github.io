Welcome to Akihiko NODA's Website!!

Akihiko NODA is Professor of Finance at the School of Commerce, Meiji University in Tokyo, Japan. He received his Ph.D. in Business and Commerce from Keio University in 2012. Before joining Meiji University in 2021, he held academic appointments at Kyoto Sangyo University, Wakayama University, and Toyo University. He has held his current professorship since April 2023.

His research focuses on the development and application of econometric methods, particularly time-series analysis and time-varying parameter models. He works on estimation and statistical inference using generalized least squares and related approaches. In financial econometrics, he applies these methods to investigate return predictability and the evolution of market efficiency across stock, foreign-exchange, commodity futures, cryptocurrency, and bond markets.

Another major area of his research is Japanese financial history, which he studies in collaboration with scholars specializing in Japanese economic and business history. Drawing on historical stock prices and rice futures data, this research examines price formation, market integration, and government intervention in prewar and wartime Japan. He also contributes to reconstructing long-run Japanese stock market indices extending back to 1924, with an emphasis on reproducible data processing.

Fields of interest

  • Econometric Methods (Time-Series Analysis)
  • Financial Econometrics
  • Financial Market Efficiency
  • Japanese Financial History

Meiji Economics Workshop

Akihiko NODA is also one of the organizers of the Meiji Economics Workshop, a research workshop at Meiji University that provides a forum for the presentation and discussion of research across a broad range of fields in economics. Information about upcoming meetings and past workshops is available on the workshop website.
