Welcome to Akihiko NODA's Website!!

Akihiko NODA is Professor of Finance at the School of Commerce, Meiji University. He received his Ph.D. in Business and Commerce from Keio University in 2012.

His research focuses on econometric methods for time-series analysis, particularly the estimation and inference of time-varying parameter models using generalized least squares. He applies these methods to study return predictability and changes in market efficiency in stock, foreign exchange, commodity futures, cryptocurrency, and bond markets.

He also studies Japanese financial history in collaboration with scholars specializing in Japanese economic and business history. Using historical stock prices and rice futures data, this research examines price formation, market integration, and government intervention in prewar and wartime Japan. His collaborative work also includes the reconstruction of Japanese stock market indices dating back to 1924.

Fields of interest

  • Econometric Methods (Time-Series Analysis)
  • Financial Econometrics
  • Financial Market Efficiency
  • Japanese Financial History

Meiji Economics Workshop

Akihiko NODA is also one of the organizers of the Meiji Economics Workshop, a research workshop at Meiji University that provides a forum for the presentation and discussion of research across a broad range of fields in economics. Information about upcoming meetings and past workshops is available on the workshop website.
