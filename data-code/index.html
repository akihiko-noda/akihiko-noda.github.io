Japanese Stock Market Indices, 1924–2026

Historical Price and Total Return Indices: Monthly Data and Reproducible R Code

The Japanese Stock Market Indices dataset provides four monthly series—Price Index (PI), Adjusted Price Index (API), and two Total Return Indices (TRI and TRI_2)—covering June 1924 through September 2026 (1,228 observations). The data combine historical stock prices, share counts, dividends, and corporate-action information with later Japanese market-index series. The dataset includes monthly returns and cumulative index levels.

Kenichi Hirayama created the original historical Excel workbooks. Akihiko Noda reconstructed the released index dataset in R using these workbooks as a reference. The associated research paper is coauthored by Hirayama, Keiichi Morimoto, and Noda.

Japanese Stock Market Indices repository (currently Private)

The repository contains the monthly CSV datasets and R code, but is not yet publicly accessible. Full reconstruction requires additional historical input workbooks and third-party index series that are not distributed there. Research use and redistribution are subject to the repository's terms.
